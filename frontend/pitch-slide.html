Deterministic NLP for early detection of common conditions
The Problem
Clinical AI tools hallucinate criteria or leak raw field names to patients. One negation bug — "No history of stroke" read as "stroke" — changes treatment plans and puts lives at risk.
The Invariants
  1. 1Negation scope terminates at clause boundaries
  2. 2Raw field names cannot reach patients (build fails)
  3. 3Ambiguous criteria surface as "unresolved," never guessed
  4. 4Emergency red-flags trigger immediate escalation
  5. 5Every stage produces auditable output before advancing
The Result
4 validated clinical pathways — DVT, Strep Throat, AFib Stroke, Pneumonia — with published probability ranges, patient-facing explanations, and doctor reports.
216 tests passing
11 intentional edge-case failures
Zero hallucination. Zero raw field leaks.
