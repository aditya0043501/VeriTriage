What This Is
This tool helps you understand how urgent your symptoms might be, using the same validated scoring systems doctors use — not an AI guess.
How It Works
  1. You describe your symptoms in your own words
  2. We identify which validated clinical score applies to your symptoms
  3. We calculate your result using the real published criteria, and show you exactly how we got there
Why This Exists
Most AI symptom checkers give an answer without showing their reasoning. Clinical scoring tools like this one are already trusted and used by real doctors. This tool makes that same trusted process accessible directly to patients, with full transparency on the reasoning and sources — and gives you a structured starting point you can bring to an actual doctor visit.
